About Us

Your Trusted Partner Since 1980

Our Legacy

Delhi Trading Company, established in 1980, is a trusted name in the field of electrical and automation products. Based in Faridabad, we have over four decades of experience in supplying high-quality industrial electrical components and providing tailored solutions to meet the evolving needs of our clients.

Our Expertise

We specialize in a wide range of electrical and automation products, working with some of the most reliable and reputed brands in the industry, including Minilec, Rishabh, Jai Balaji, Vaishno Hotline, Esbee, BCH, Elmeasure, and Klipco.

Our Services

Our product portfolio caters to various industrial sectors, ensuring quality, performance, and durability. In addition to trading, we also offer custom panel building services, enabling us to deliver complete and customized electrical solutions under one roof.

Our Commitment

Over the years, we have earned the trust of OEMs, contractors, and industrial customers by consistently delivering genuine products, technical expertise, and timely service. At Delhi Trading Company, we believe in building long-term relationships based on quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction which sets us apart.
